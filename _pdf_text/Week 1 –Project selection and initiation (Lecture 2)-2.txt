Selection and initiation
      Proper project selection is crucial for meeting the business's needs. Inadequate selection processes
      significantly reduce the chances of project success.

      To increase the likelihood of project success, in the initial phase, we should ：


 ● Analyse Business Needs and Strategic Direction
       ○ Understand the organization's goals and objectives.
       ○ Align the project with the strategic direction.

 ● Determine Project Nature and Scope

       ○ Define the project's boundaries and deliverables.
       ○ Identify key requirements and constraints.

 ● Evaluate Project Value to the Business
       ○ Assess the financial implications and potential returns.
       ○ Develop a business case.
Establishing an Overall Picture
 To establish a comprehensive understanding of the project, consider the following key areas:

  ❖ Evaluating Business Value
        ➢ Alignment with Strategic Plans:
               ■   How does the project align with the long-term strategic goals of the business?
        ➢ Potential Benefits:
               ■   Will the project introduce a new product?
               ■   Can it improve customer support?
               ■   Will it enhance productivity?
               ■   Is it likely to reduce operational costs?
  ❖ Defining Project Nature and Scope
        ➢ Cost Justification:
               ■   Are the costs of the project, including development and support, justified by the expected benefits?
        ➢ Measurable Benefits:
               ■   What specific, measurable advantages does the project offer compared to its expenditures?
  ❖ Conducting Risk Assessment
         ➢ Risk Identification:
               ■   Is the project associated with high risks?
         ➢ Mitigation Strategies:
               ■   Identify potential risks and develop strategies to mitigate them.
         ➢ Anticipated Challenges:
               ■   What challenges or issues could arise during the project?
What is a Business Case

 ● Purpose: Helps decision-makers
   determine if a project is worth pursuing
   and secure approval and funding.
 ● Value: Ensures the project aligns with
   strategic goals and provides a foundation
   for planning and execution.
 ● Content: Includes an executive
   summary, problem statement, analysis of
   options, cost-benefit analysis, risk
   assessment, implementation plan, and
   conclusion with recommendations.
 ● Target Audience: Executives, senior
   management, project sponsors,
   stakeholders, and project managers.
Reasons for choosing the wrong projects?
 Several major reasons why organizations choose the wrong projects include:
 ❖ Bias and Errors in Judgment
      ➢ Subjective preferences and personal biases affecting decisions.
 ❖ Ineffective Project Portfolio Management
      ➢ Failure to establish a robust framework for managing project portfolios.
 ❖ Lack of Proper Metrics
      ➢ Absence of accurate metrics for evaluating project value.
 ❖ Inadequate Risk Assessment
      ➢ Inability to properly assess and value project risks.
 ❖ Misidentification of Efficient Project Portfolios
       ➢ Failure to identify and select projects that align with the "efficient frontier.”
Strategic Planning

A strategic plan is a document used by company leaders to outline the company's long-term vision,
goals, and objectives, typically focusing on a timeframe of 3-5 years.



The development of strategies (projects) must focus on what is needed by the organisation to meet
the strategic plan’s goals and objectives

Ask Yourself:
Does the proposed project deliver a product or service that aligns with the objectives defined in the
strategic plan?
Strategic Planning: SWOT analysis
▪ A commonly used tool to build a strategic plan is the SWOT analysis. It is an
  information-gathering technique that evaluates external influences against internal
  capabilities.

▪ Strengths: Internal attributes that support achieving objectives.

▪ Weaknesses: Internal attributes that hinder achieving objectives.

▪ Opportunities: External factors that the organization can capitalize on.

▪ Threats: External factors that could jeopardize the organization's success.

Step 3 of Web Design: Building Your Web
with React.js
React.js is a popular JavaScript library developed and
maintained by Facebook that has many features that make it
ideal for building modern front-end applications. Here are the
key features of React.js:
 -   Componentized development: React.js uses a componentized
     development model to split the application into multiple
     independent, reusable components. Each component has its own
     state and behaviour and can be nested and combined.

 -   Virtual DOM: React.js introduces the concept of a virtual DOM,
     which is a lightweight copy of the actual DOM similar to the
     browser's. When data changes, React improves rendering
     performance and efficiency by comparing the differences between
     the virtual DOM and the actual DOM and minimally updating the
     actual DOM.
